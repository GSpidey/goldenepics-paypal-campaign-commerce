## Narration

Turning a business idea into a campaign should be simple. Paying for the creative work should be just as easy.

GoldenEpics Studio brings AI campaign creation and PayPal checkout into one workspace for small businesses and independent creators.

Start by describing what you want to promote, who you want to reach, and what you want your audience to do. AI turns that brief into a creative proposal with campaign hooks and a visual direction.

Review the proposal, see exactly what the package includes, and check the price before purchasing.

Here, the nine-dollar Campaign Starter package is purchased through PayPal Sandbox. Choose PayPal, approve the purchase, and return to your campaign. The app confirms the payment and enables creation of your AI campaign package.

Your purchase produces three campaign hooks, a thirty-second script, three social captions, a carousel outline, and a visual production brief.

Read the finished package in your workspace, download it for editing, and use it to guide your next promotion. Your brief, purchase, and deliverables stay together, so you can return to the same campaign and collect your work.

PayPal gives customers a familiar way to purchase AI creative services. GoldenEpics connects that checkout directly to the work they ordered, bringing proposal, payment, and delivery into one clear journey.

GoldenEpics Studio. Choose your campaign. Pay with PayPal. Bring your idea to life.
